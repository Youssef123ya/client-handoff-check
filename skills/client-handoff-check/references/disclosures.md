## Models and data handling
Use the processing available through the supported Uliya experts. Prefer an available local model, but model availability and offline behavior have not been established. Do not claim offline-only processing. No external endpoint, model credential or custom model is configured by this Skill.
Limit input to the approved brief and delivery inventory. Save only the exact approved report at a resolved destination outside the delivery tree. Platform permissions and data-security rules remain authoritative. If platform processing would require an unauthorized external data flow, pause and explain the boundary rather than proceeding.
Use synthetic data for hackathon testing and recording.

## Validation status and known limits
The deliverable is an authored orchestration Skill. Local static checks and their diagnostics are maintained outside this runtime package. They establish document and mapping compliance only. Installation, actual model behavior, expert output shape, file-type support, permission handling, save collision behavior and device execution require validation on the assigned NAS.
Exact-name matches do not prove content validity or creative quality. Broad requirements need human judgment. Enumeration may be limited or incomplete; inventories are not atomic. No compatibility, performance or success-rate claim is made before NAS testing.
